Sources & demo details

The map uses all 7,496 nuclei and original author UMAP coordinates from GSE270392. It shows gene-expression similarity, not physical positions in the root.

The result, review, recall and activity belong to one recorded Celloras experiment. The marker reference is associated with the same study, so this demonstrates the workflow, not independent annotation accuracy.

The review is explicitly illustrative and keeps the root-hair identity provisional. Missing matches do not establish novelty. Current comparison uses marker evidence; direct expression comparison between studies and broader plant coverage are future work.

Nightshift investigations run on NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra; three completed on the live app on 27 October. The displayed workflow uses Codex through OpenShell.
